### Gift Design Principles

This project should feel personally made rather than procedurally generated.

1. The recipient should discover rather than consume.
2. Not every interaction should be explicitly signposted.
3. Recurring motifs should connect the worlds.
4. The environment should remember meaningful interactions.
5. Humor should dominate the early experience; intimacy should emerge gradually.
6. The Letter should provide a deliberate quiet contrast to the chaotic sections.
7. Imperfections should feel handmade, not unfinished.
8. The three worlds should feel like parts of the same universe.
9. Exploration should be rewarded with small details rather than conventional progress mechanics.
10. The final experience should leave the recipient with something memorable that exists beyond the interaction itself.

### Core test

At every design decision, ask:

> "Would this make the recipient feel that someone specifically made this for them?"

If the answer is no, reconsider the interaction.
